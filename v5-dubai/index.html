FAQ

What people ask before a demo.

Which POS does VILAX work with?

Syrve, cloud only: on-premise servers are not supported. Losses and the shift review are calculated from Syrve. Plans are set right in VILAX, and reports export to Excel.

How long does connection take?

It depends on your setup — the manager will name the timeline after the demo. With annual billing, standard onboarding is included: one supported connection, user access set-up and a kick-off session for your team.

Who sees my venue's data?

Only the people you give access to. Access is set by role: a manager sees the shift, the owner sees everything.

Can I start with Core only?

Yes. Core is required, the blocks are optional — add Guests 360, Finance & Control, Loyalty or a team block when you have a job for it.

What's in All-in?

Core, all four blocks and 50 employees for 2,990 AED a month before VAT. Each employee above 50 costs 25 AED a month.

How much does VILAX cost?

Per venue per month, before VAT: Core — 490 AED, Guests 360 — 800 AED, Finance & Control — 549 AED, Loyalty — 365 AED. Team: Academy — 11 AED per active employee, no minimum; People + Academy — 25 AED per employee, minimum 500 AED a month. All-in — 2,990 AED.

How does billing work?

Monthly or a year in advance. Annual billing saves 10 % and includes standard onboarding. Prices are before VAT; VAT 5 % is added on top. Telephony, WhatsApp and SMS are billed at your provider's rates.

How much does AI cost?

The Coordinator is included in Core with 500 requests a month. Aura in Guests 360 includes 1,500 guest conversations a month. Need more? Aura +1,000 conversations for 150 AED, Coordinator +500 requests for 100 AED. Packs last until the end of the month, with no automatic charges.

Is there an app?

No separate app is needed: VILAX works in the browser on a computer and on a phone, and summaries arrive in Telegram.
